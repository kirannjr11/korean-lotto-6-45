What we measure

Frequency rates use the number of draws as their denominator. Main and bonus numbers are counted separately. Hot and quiet rankings use the most recent draws within your selected range.

Each draw contributes 15 pairs and 20 triples. All 990 possible pairs and 14,190 possible triples are included in the downloadable project reports, including combinations that never appeared.

“Previous overlap” compares the main set with the preceding draw in the selected range. The first selected draw has no predecessor.

What the evidence means

Draws are unordered sets. Sorted results do not tell us the order in which balls were extracted.

Comparing many combinations will naturally reveal unusual-looking patterns. A high past count or a lead over one random backtest does not establish predictive power.

This workspace operates on the saved local dataset. Source URLs, raw responses and verification coverage are included in the project. Restart the server after refreshing data.
